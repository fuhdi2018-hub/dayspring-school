☀ Welcome to Dayspring School

This is a free learning plan for young children (Nursery to Primary 5). There are no fees, no exams, and no grades.

Every child learns to read 📖, write ✏️, and speak 💬 at their own pace. You can track each child, write simple notes, and print daily lessons — everything stays on this device.

How this app works

  1. ☀ Home — the idea behind Dayspring, plus today's verse.
  2. 🌱 Journey — what one child's path looks like.
  3. 📖 Lesson — today's skill, handiwork, and reflection. Print it for class.
  4. 👧🏾 Children — add each child; tap what they are working on right now (📖 reading, ✏️ writing, or 💬 speaking); jot a short note; log milestones and celebrate them 🎉.
  5. 🏫 Class — everyone at a glance.
  6. 🧺 Showcase — hands-on work children practiced.
  7. 🤝 Guide — how to group a child, and why there are no grades.
