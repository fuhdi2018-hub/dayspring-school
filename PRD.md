# Dayspring School — A Free Model for Christian Early Education

## Vision
A free, pressure-free model for Christian education from Nursery
to Primary 5, built on one goal: every child learns to read,
write, and communicate — because once they can, they can know
Christ for themselves. No child is ranked, segregated, or labeled.

## Core Principles
1. **Free — no fees, no payment, ever.**
2. **No grade hierarchy** — Nursery through Primary 5 is one
   continuous journey, not separate walled-off classes.
3. **Flexible ability grouping** — children move through loose
   groups based on what they can currently do, not fixed to one
   subject or one label. A child can move groups as they grow.
4. **Core foundation, kept simple** — reading, writing,
   communication are the center; no cumbersome subject overload.
5. **Vocation and skills from the start** — practical, hands-on
   skills taught alongside the core from the youngest ages.
6. **One goal, equal for all** — every child reaches the same
   destination, without being told they are more or less capable.
7. **Knowledge of Christ and denial of flesh** — woven through
   daily learning, not a separate "religious subject."

## MVP Scope (single local prototype)
- Home / Model Overview — explains the vision and principles.
- Sample Child Journey — one example child's current ability
  group in plain, non-ranked language.
- Sample Daily Lesson — a core skill + a vocational activity +
  a reflection on Christ.

## Added Features (Phase 2)
- **Ability-group tracker** — add multiple children, move them
  between groups (Growing in Reading / Writing / Speaking) as
  they progress. Saved locally in the browser, no database.
- **Simple progress notes** — free-text notes per child instead
  of scores or grades.
- **Daily Scripture + reflection rotation** — a small built-in
  list of short verses/reflections that rotates day to day.
- **Skills showcase** — a gallery area for a child's hands-on
  work (drawing, description of a skill practiced).
- **"How to group a child" guide** — a short explainer page for
  teachers on flexible, non-labeling ability grouping.
- **Printable daily lesson card** — formats the day's lesson for
  printing, for classrooms with limited device access.

All features run with no backend, no paid service, and no
sign-in — data is saved in the browser (localStorage).

## Out of Scope (for now)
Accounts, payments, multi-device sync, full curriculum.
